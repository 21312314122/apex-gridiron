How to Play

Controls

  • WASD or Arrow Keys — Move the controlled player (QB, RB, or receiver after catch)
  • Space — Snap the ball / Throw the ball (when aiming) / Handoff
  • Mouse — Aim pass direction and power (drag style optional; click target)
  • 1-6 / Click plays — Select formation & play before the snap
  • Esc — Pause / Menu

Core Loop

  • Earn money only when you win a game (you set the win bonus amount in settings). Higher fan happiness can boost the bonus.
  • Losses only hurt your record (W-L). Money is safe on losses.
  • Fan Happiness rises with wins (especially blowouts) and falls with losses. Affects atmosphere and slight money bonus.
  • Regular season is 10 games. 6+ wins → Playoffs (Wild Card → Divisional → Conference → Super Bowl).
  • Spend money in Free Agency or use the Trade Center to upgrade the roster.
  • Career Stats track Super Bowls, wins, TDs, yards across seasons.
  • Local Multiplayer (hotseat) lets two players share a keyboard and alternate offense.
  • Last play is recorded for Replay viewing after games.
  • Call pass plays (Verticals, Slants, Posts, Curls, Deep, Flood) or run plays.

Scoring

  • Touchdown = 7 (auto PAT for simplicity)
  • Field Goal not implemented in this version (focus on TD drives)
  • 4 downs to gain 10 yards or score

Ideas to Make It More Eventful

  • ✓ Fan happiness — Meter that rises/falls with results and affects bonuses.
  • ✓ Playoffs & Super Bowl — 10-game regular season → Wild Card → Divisional → Conference → Super Bowl.
  • ✓ Trade system — Propose 1-for-1 trades with any NFL team; AI accepts based on overall value.
  • ✓ Career stats — Super Bowls, wins, losses, TDs, yards, playoff appearances tracked across seasons.
  • ✓ Local multiplayer — Hotseat 2-player on the same device.
  • ✓ Replay system — Last play is recorded and can be rewatched after the game.
  • Full playable defense — Switch to control a linebacker or safety, user-controlled blitzes and coverage.
  • Weather system — Rain/snow reduces grip & speed, wind affects deep balls and kicks.
  • Injuries & fatigue — Players get dinged; manage depth chart and rest starters.
  • Draft & salary cap — Real salary-cap pressure, rookies, contract years, holdouts.
  • Scheme customization — West Coast, Air Raid, Power Run, RPO packages.
  • Special teams — Kickoffs, punts, FG, returns with risk/reward.
  • Online multiplayer — Needs a backend (Firebase / WebSockets) — not pure static HTML.
  • Custom plays editor — Draw your own routes and save playbooks.
  • Dynamic difficulty — AI adjusts based on your record and skill.
  • Team logos / uniform editor — Full visual customization.
  • Season simulation — Sim remaining games when you want to skip.
